#### -[ Intro

<PRE>
History has a logging problem.

Ask anyone who was around about a bug they found. They won't start with the
CVE number. They'll tell you where they were sitting, what they were
drinking at the time, who they called first.

Phrack World History is for the stories behind the hacks. The ones still
sitting in chat logs and data dumps, or only in the heads of the people who
were part of it.

In this issue we have an SSH bug that handed out root across a scary chunk
of the Internet. A one line change that nearly slipped a backdoor into the
Linux kernel. Blueboxers who worked the exchanges one tone at a time, and a
proxy in some old malware that marked the moment computer viruses turned
into a product.

So let us know if you were there! You don't need to be famous for this. Send
proof if you still have it and tell us what it felt like when it all
clicked.

Boxes get wiped, domains expire, and people log off for one last time.
Before more of it disappears, send us your story.

Hack the record.

The Phrack Staff
